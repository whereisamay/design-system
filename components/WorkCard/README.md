# WorkCard

The card for one piece of work: an essay, a case study, a project.

**Markup:** `<article class="work-card">` with `.work-card-media` (an `<img>`, or the striped placeholder with a `+`) and `.work-card-body` holding a `.chip`, an `<h3>`, a `<p>` and a `.work-card-link`.

- 2px `ink` border, `radius-md`, `cream` fill. On hover it rises 6px, tilts −0.6° and throws the hard `shadow-lift`.
- Media is 16:10. With no photo, the `mustard` / `mustard-2` diagonal stripes stand in.
- Lay cards out three across (two at tablet, one on phones), 28px apart.
- One chip, a title of up to six words, one or two lines of description.
